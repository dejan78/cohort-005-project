# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary fixes the words used by the product, the code, support and finance staff, and every email the learner receives, so that all of them mean the same thing.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A person who has bought a course. "Student" is only the name of their account role.
_Avoid_: Student (outside the role name), customer, buyer

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member. Only an Admin may approve or decline a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Reviewer, support (as a role)

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's request, with a written reason, to get their money back for a Purchase. An Admin approves or declines every one by hand. It is never instant. A Purchase can have at most one, and a learner cannot withdraw it once raised.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, reversal, chargeback

**Refund**:
The money returned to the learner once a Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback, cancellation

**Refunded**:
The state of a Purchase whose Refund Request was approved. The learner no longer has access to the course.
_Avoid_: Cancelled, reversed, voided

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase may have a Refund Request raised against it. It must be inside the Refund Window, have under two hours of video watched, have under half of the course's lessons completed, not be a claimed team seat, and never have had a Refund Request before, whether open, approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, cancellable

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen whole calendar days after a Purchase, counting the day of purchase, in which a Refund Request may be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refundable Amount**:
The amount a learner would get back if their Refund Request were approved: the price they actually paid for a purchasing-power price, otherwise the course's list price.
_Avoid_: Amount paid (when meaning the refund figure)

**Refunded Amount**:
The amount actually returned to the learner after a Refund Request is approved. A declined request has none.

### Refund emails

**Refund Request Confirmation**:
The email that tells the learner their Refund Request was received and will be reviewed. It does not mean the request was approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal email, cancellation confirmation

**Refund Approved notification**:
The email that tells the learner their Refund Request was approved and the Refunded Amount is on its way.

**Refund Declined notification**:
The email that tells the learner their Refund Request was declined.

### Reserved terms

**Chargeback**:
A payment dispute the cardholder raises through their bank rather than through Cadence. Never use it for a Refund Request.

**Reversal**:
Voiding a card payment before it settles. Never use it for a Refund or Refund Request.
